Arcanomaly privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Arcanomaly is a game made by MCVC Games, an independent developer in Türkiye. This page explains what the game does with information about you. Questions go to mcvcgamesdev@gmail.com.

In short: the current version of Arcanomaly works offline and does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved in the game's own storage on your phone or tablet. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device's settings, deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • It needs no account and asks for no name, email or other contact details.
  • It shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google handles that under its own privacy policy. Google Play gives developers summary statistics, such as install numbers, and crash reports from players who allow them; we use these only to understand how the game is doing and to fix problems.

Features that will change this policy

Planned features such as rewarded ads, an optional Google Play Games sign-in and in-app purchases through Google Play involve outside services. Before any of them reaches the game, this policy will be updated to name each service, what it collects and why, and the date at the top will change.

Children

Arcanomaly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and contacting us

We hold no personal data about you through the game, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Changes

Any change is posted on this page with a new effective date.
