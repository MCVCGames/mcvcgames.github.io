Arcanomaly privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026 (second version: play statistics and crash reports added)

Arcanomaly is a game made by MCVC Games, an independent developer in Türkiye. This page explains what the game does with information about you. Questions go to mcvcgamesdev@gmail.com.

In short: Arcanomaly plays offline and needs no account. If you allow it, the game sends anonymous play statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, which we use only to improve the game. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved in the game's own storage on your phone or tablet. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device's settings, deletes them.

Play statistics and crash reports

When "Share play data" is on in Settings (and, where the law asks for it, after you agree in the consent message), the game sends the following to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), which processes it for us:

  • How you play: which screens you open, the runs you start and finish (chapter, difficulty, how long, whether you won), the cards and tools you pick, rewards and in-game currency earned and spent, settings you change, and offers you watch for a reward.
  • How the game runs: your phone's model and system version, the game's version, frame rate and memory use, and crash reports with the state of the game when it crashed.
  • Random identifiers that Firebase creates for this installation of the game, so that statistics can be counted per player. They are not linked to your name, email or any account.
  • Approximate location: Google derives your country from a masked version of your IP address. More precise location is switched off.

We use this to see where players get stuck, how long runs take, which parts are fun or too hard, and what makes the game crash. Google keeps the player-level statistics for 14 months.

The consent message

For players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before sending anything, and you can say no without losing any part of the game. To find out whether the message is needed where you are, the game asks Google's consent service when it starts; like any internet request, this tells Google your device's IP address. Your choice is stored on your device, and you can change it at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

What the game does not do

  • It needs no account and asks for no name, email or other contact details.
  • It does not use your device's advertising ID; the game removes Android's permission for it.
  • It shows no personalised ads to anyone, and it does not ask for your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • It does not sell your data or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Google

When you download the game from Google Play, and when the game uses Firebase or the consent service, Google handles that data under its privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google Play also gives developers summary statistics, such as install numbers.

Features that will change this policy

Rewarded ads, an optional Google Play Games sign-in and in-app purchases through Google Play are planned. The library Google uses for ads is already part of the game because the consent message comes with it, but no ads are requested yet. Before any of these features reaches players, this policy will be updated to name each service, what it collects and why, and the date at the top will change.

Children

Arcanomaly is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the game shows no personalised ads to anyone.

Your choices and rights

Turn off "Share play data" in Settings, under Privacy, and the game stops sending statistics and crash reports. Uninstalling the game removes the random identifiers from your device. Because we never learn your name or contact details through the game, we cannot look up your statistics by who you are; if you have a question or a request about your data, email us and we will help as far as we can link data to your device.

Changes

Any change is posted on this page with a new effective date.
